## The business (confirmed by Vivek)

- Family-owned tannery in **Ranipet, Tamil Nadu**. Partners: Vivek (on paper so far) and his father (runs operations).
- **Wet unit + dry unit.** Takes salted hides through to finished leather. Focus: finished leather, e.g. cow nappa with flashy/printed finishes.
- **Capacity:** about 50,000 sq ft. Per month is assumed — still to confirm.
- **Model today:** job-work subcontractor. Finishes leather for 1–2 clients (finished-leather suppliers and exporters). Revenue is very low and capacity utilization roughly 5% (estimate).
- **Job-work selling rates by finish:** about ₹35/sq ft (simple) up to ₹55 / ₹75 / ₹100 for complex finishes.
- **Compliance:** TNPCB compliant; member of a CETP; export license (IEC) and CLE membership in place.
- **No LWG certification.** Deliberately deferred until the business improves.
- **No shop, no website, no online presence** beyond a Google Business Profile.
- No in-house goods production; small goods orders can be done through the network.
- **Vivek's time:** limited weekdays (full-time job), more on weekends. Growth budget: about ₹1L for 90 days.
- **Goal:** revive a near-sick unit into a systemised, revenue-generating business, and explore all revenue streams.

## Strategy (proposed by Claude, direction accepted)

The constraint is **demand/sales, not production**. Idle capacity means extra volume is close to pure contribution.

1. **Track A (now): fill idle capacity with job work** from tanneries, finished-leather suppliers and exporters in Ranipet / Melvisharam / Vellore / Ambur / Chennai.
   - No working capital needed.
   - Offer: rate card per finish, swatch kit, turnaround guarantee, free 200–500 sq ft trial.
2. **Track B (from week 4): export finished leather on own account**, only against confirmed orders with an advance or LC. Never build stock.
   - Channels: CLE buyer-seller meets and trade inquiries, IILF Chennai (Feb 2027), direct outreach to non-LWG mid-size manufacturers abroad.
3. **Parked:** online/D2C leather goods. No product capability, high CAC, eats founder time. Revisit after the milestone.

**Milestone (provisional):** 10,000 sq ft/month (about 20% utilization) and 2 export samples approved by end of Feb 2027. Spending scales only after this is hit.
