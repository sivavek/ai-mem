## Better lead sources (priority order)

1. **CETP member list** (HIQ is a member). The CETP office has every small and mid tannery in the cluster. Father to request it.
2. **Finished-leather suppliers/traders** on IndiaMART (Vellore / Ambur / Chennai): they resell, and many outsource finishing.
3. **Leather-goods manufacturers** in Ambur, Vaniyambadi and Chennai, who buy finished leather directly.
4. **CLE** member directory / trade inquiries / buyer-seller meets (export).
5. **IILF Chennai, Feb 2027**: walk the halls with the swatch kit.
